# Pre-registration: strategy batch 4, diversifiers (2026-11-04)

Written and committed **before** any batch-4 result is computed. Judged by the three-tier
framework (`docs/prereg/2026-11-03-decision-framework.md`). Trials added: 30 (running
total 395).

## Why these three

Every surviving strategy so far is the same idea (hourly volatility breakout, a
trend-following entry). Batch 4 tests ideas that make money at **different** moments:
buying weakness inside an uptrend, buying panic in the futures market, and earning the
bid-ask spread. A strategy that passes here is valuable even with modest returns,
because it would not lose at the same time as breakout.

Sizing is deliberately a little more aggressive than batch 2: the volatility target is
80% a year (as in the vol-sized breakout), not 60%.

## Shared setup for strategies 7 and 8 (as batch 1)

- **Universe:** BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, LINK on Binance.US, daily bars. Long or flat.
  Decided on a day's close, filled at the next day's open.
- **Costs per side:** BTC 5, ETH 5, SOL 6, XRP 5, DOGE 7, LINK 8 bps, ×3 on days flagged
  as top-decile volatility (batch 1's daily stress flags).
- **Weight per entry:** (1/6) × min(1, 0.80 / annualized 30-day realized volatility),
  fixed at entry.
- **Holding:** a fixed number of days M, then exit. No new entry in a coin while it is
  held. One position per coin.
- **Selection:** batch 1's walk-forward (`walk_forward` in `src/research/batch1.py`):
  quarterly folds 2023–2024, each choosing the grid point with the best Sharpe over the
  previous 12 months; the final setting is the best Sharpe over calendar 2024.
- **Out-of-sample:** 2025-01-01 → 2026-11-01, run once with the final setting. Tier rules
  as in the decision framework; buy-and-hold reference is the equal-weight 6 coins.

## Strategy 7: buy the dip in an uptrend (`dip_buy`)

- **Trend filter:** the day's close is above its L-day simple moving average.
- **Dip:** the k-day return, divided by (30-day daily return volatility × √k), is at or
  below −Z. In words: a drop of at least Z "normal moves" over k days.
- **Entry:** both conditions true at a day's close → buy at the next open, hold M days.
- **Grid:** L ∈ {50, 100} × k ∈ {1, 3} × Z ∈ {1.5, 2.0} × M ∈ {3, 7} → 16 trials.
- Can reach any tier.

## Strategy 8: buy panic in the futures market (`funding_fear`)

- **Signal:** each coin's Hyperliquid funding rate, averaged over the last A days
  (A ∈ {1, 3}), is below its own q-th percentile of the trailing 90 days of A-day
  averages (q ∈ {5, 10}). Needs at least 60 days of history. In words: futures traders
  are unusually short or unusually unwilling to pay to be long.
- **Entry:** signal true at a day's close → buy spot at the next open, hold M days,
  M ∈ {7, 14}.
- **Grid:** A × q × M → 8 trials.
- **Data limit:** funding history starts 2023-05-12 (XRP 2023-06-18), so the first
  signals are in mid-2023 and the early 2023 folds have no trades. That is accepted.
- Can reach any tier.

## Strategy 9: passive market making (`passive_mm`)

- **Coins:** XRP, DOGE, LINK on Binance.US USDT, the pairs with spreads wide enough to
  earn (measured 1.6, 6.8, 7.4 bps). The account's maker fee is 0%.
- **Data:** every Binance.US trade on these pairs from the live collector,
  2026-09-29 → 2026-11-03 (about 300–1,400 trades a day).
- **Fair price:** exponential average of trade prices with a 5-minute half-life,
  computed only from trades at least 2 seconds older than the moment being simulated
  (quote update delay).
- **Quotes:** a buy order at fair × (1 − h) and a sell order at fair × (1 + h), each for
  one lot of $10. Inventory is limited to C lots long or short of the starting point.
  At the limit, the side that would add more stops quoting. Since spot can't go short,
  the strategy starts holding C lots of each coin, and its result is measured against
  simply holding those C lots (so price moves of the starting inventory don't count).
- **Fills (conservative):** our buy fills only if a trade prints **below** our price;
  our sell fills only if a trade prints **above** it. Touching our price is not a fill
  (we assume we are last in the queue). Fill size = min(lot, that trade's size). Filled
  at our quoted price.
- **End:** any inventory away from the starting C lots is closed at the last trade price
  minus/plus the coin's per-side cost (XRP 5, DOGE 7, LINK 8 bps).
- **Grid:** h ∈ {10, 20, 40} bps × C ∈ {1, 3} → 6 trials, the same setting for all three
  coins.
- **Selection:** 2026-09-29 → 2026-10-16: highest total profit per dollar of maximum
  capital (C × $10 × 2 × 3 coins).
- **Test:** 2026-10-16 → 2026-11-03, run once.
- **Pass (to watch):** test profit > 0; still > 0 when every fill is charged an extra
  2 bps (adverse selection stress); and profitable on at least 2 of the 3 coins.
- **Cap:** 18 test days and a fill model that only sees trades, not the order book. The
  best possible outcome is **watch**, which means a shadow run in chorus-howl that posts
  real-time simulated quotes against the live order book.

## Decision

- Per strategy: retire / watch / candidate by the framework (7, 8) or the rule above (9).
- Survivors at watch or better get a shadow run in chorus-howl with frozen settings.

## Things decided in advance

- No grid, window, cost or rule changes after seeing any result. Bugs found after running
  are fixed and both results are reported.
- Code: `src/research/batch4.py`. Results: `docs/2026-11-04-batch4-results.md` and rows in
  `docs/research-index.json`.

## Why these could fail (stated beforehand)

- Dip-buying in 2025–26 meets a falling market; the trend filter may keep it mostly in
  cash, or let it buy dips that keep falling.
- Funding extremes are rare: perhaps a handful of signals per coin per year, so results
  rest on few trades.
- Market making on thin pairs: few fills, and the ones we get may be the ones right before
  the price moves through us (adverse selection). The trade-only fill model can't see
  queue position or hidden liquidity.
