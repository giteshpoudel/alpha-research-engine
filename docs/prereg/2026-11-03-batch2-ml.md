# Pre-registration: strategy batch 2, machine learning (2026-11-03)

Written and committed **before** any batch-2 result is computed. Judged by the three-tier
framework (`docs/prereg/2026-11-03-decision-framework.md`). Scoping: section 4 of
`docs/2026-11-03-strategy-scoping.md`. Trials added: 12 (running total 349).

## Shared setup

- **Universe, costs, execution:** as batch 1. BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, LINK (Binance.US);
  long or flat; weights ≥ 0 summing to ≤ 1; decided on a bar's close, filled at the next
  open; cost per side BTC 5, ETH 5, SOL 6, XRP 5, DOGE 7, LINK 8 bps, ×3 on top-decile
  volatility bars.
- **Model:** scikit-learn `HistGradientBoostingClassifier(max_depth=d, learning_rate=0.05,
  max_iter=200, min_samples_leaf=20, l2_regularization=1.0, random_state=0)`. One model
  pooled across the six coins. Missing feature values are allowed (the model handles them).
- **Retraining (purged walk-forward):** the model is retrained at the start of every
  calendar quarter on all samples **whose label was fully known before that quarter
  started**, from 2022-01 onward. In-sample test quarters: 2023Q1–2024Q4. The same
  quarterly retraining continues through the out-of-sample window with frozen
  hyperparameters, so no model ever sees data from its own test quarter.
- **Hyperparameter choice:** the (θ, depth) pair with the best net Sharpe of the stitched
  in-sample test quarters 2023Q1–2024Q4.
- **Out-of-sample:** 2025-01-01 → 2026-11-01, run once.

## Strategy 5: meta-labeling the breakout (`meta_breakout`)

- **Primary signals:** the frozen breakout selections used in batch 1 (1h bars).
- **One sample per breakout entry.** Label = 1 if the trade's net return (entry and exit
  costs included) is > 0. A label is known at the trade's exit.
- **Features at the entry bar's close:** realized vol over 24h and 720h (annualized) and
  their ratio; returns over 24h, 72h, 168h; close vs its 720h average; 24h volume vs the
  720h average hourly volume ×24; ATR(N)/close; hour of day; BTC returns over 24h and 168h;
  share of the six coins with a positive 168h return; trailing 72h Hyperliquid funding,
  annualized.
- **Rule:** take the entry only if the model's probability ≥ θ. Size and exit as the
  vol-sized breakout: (1/6) × min(1, 0.8 / 720h vol), fixed at entry, breakout exit rules.
- **Grid:** θ ∈ {0.40, 0.50, 0.60} × depth ∈ {2, 3} → 6 trials.
- **Extra condition:** the filter must beat the unfiltered vol-sized breakout on the same
  OOS window: higher Sharpe than 0.90 **and** a smaller max drawdown than −20.1%.
  Otherwise it is retired as "adds nothing".
- **Cap:** the primary signals had been seen out of sample, so this can reach at most the
  **watch** tier.

## Strategy 6: gradient-boosted daily coin model (`gbm_daily`)

- **One sample per coin per day** (daily bars). Label = 1 if the return from the next
  day's open to the open three days later is > 0. Known three days after the decision.
- **Features at the day's close:** returns over 1, 3, 7, 14, 28 days; realized vol over
  7 and 30 days and their ratio; 7-day vs 30-day average volume; close vs its 28-day high;
  close vs its 50-day average; BTC returns over 7 and 28 days; share of the six coins with
  a positive 28-day return; trailing 3-day Hyperliquid funding, annualized; day of week.
- **Rule:** each day, hold a coin if its probability ≥ θ, at weight
  (1/6) × min(1, 0.6 / 30-day realized vol). Re-decided daily.
- **Grid:** θ ∈ {0.50, 0.55, 0.60} × depth ∈ {2, 3} → 6 trials.
- Can reach any tier.

## Decision (per strategy)

- **Retire:** OOS net return ≤ 0, Sharpe < 0.5, or OOS return ≤ 0 with costs ×2 (plus the
  extra condition for strategy 5).
- **Candidate:** not retired, budget max drawdown ≤ 15%, max drawdown ≤ half that of
  equal-weight buy-and-hold over the OOS window, longest underwater ≤ 182 days.
- **Watch:** otherwise.

Reported regardless: in-sample walk-forward, OOS AUC and accuracy of the model, the share of
breakout entries taken (strategy 5), feature importances of the last in-sample model,
costs ×2, buy-and-hold, the 2025-10-10 crash and the Binance hack windows.
